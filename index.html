1. Information Nidhi uses

Nidhi is a personal income, expense and budget-management app. It uses information you enter, import or permit it to read to maintain your ledger, calculate spending limits, display History and Stats, manage recurring income and commitments, identify potential duplicates, and provide reminders.

  • Financial records: amounts, income/expense type, dates, categories, category icons, account labels, notes, descriptions and payment references. Account or UPI identifiers can be present in information you enter or import.
  • Budget settings: regular income sources, commitments, amounts, paid/credited status, linked transactions and monthly plans.
  • Local profile: your chosen nickname. If you use Google sign-in, Nidhi can also store the name, email address and profile-photo URL supplied by Google.
  • Preferences: appearance, selected payment apps and bank senders, reminders, summary times, backup settings, app-lock choices, and whether introductory guidance has been shown.
  • Import bookkeeping: local transaction links and message fingerprints used to avoid processing the same event repeatedly. These are not used for advertising or profiling.

A nickname creates a local profile, not a ledger account on a developer-operated server. Nidhi does not need your bank login password, banking PIN or OTP to track transactions. Do not enter those secrets into notes or support messages.

2. Optional bank SMS reading

Purpose: SMS-based money management—recognizing bank debit and credit alerts to help record income and expenses. SMS reading is off by default.

To enable it, open Settings → Permissions & notifications → Automatic transactions · SMS, review the in-app disclosure, grant the requested SMS permissions, and choose bank senders. READ_SMS supports importing existing messages; RECEIVE_SMS supports recognizing new incoming alerts, including while Nidhi is not open.

Android SMS permissions can expose inbox content and incoming messages. Existing-message import reads this month’s inbox and applies bank-sender and financial-message filters on your device. Incoming alerts are filtered on-device as well. The app is designed to ignore nonmatching messages, OTPs, verification codes, promotional content, failed or pending payments, and unclear alerts.

Nidhi saves recognized transaction details, such as amount, direction, time, reference, source or masked account information, and a duplicate-detection fingerprint. It does not save the complete raw SMS body as a ledger record or upload raw SMS history to the developer. Parsed ledger entries can be included in an export or optional Google Drive backup you enable.

Turn this feature off in Nidhi and/or revoke SMS permission in Android settings to stop future SMS importing. Previously imported entries remain until you delete them. Nidhi does not send SMS, act as your default messaging app, or request Call Log access.

3. Optional payment-notification reading

Notification reading is off by default. Enable it through Settings → Permissions & notifications → Automatic transactions · Notifications, review the disclosure, grant notification access in Android, and choose the payment or banking apps to process.

Android notification access is broad and can expose sensitive notification content. Nidhi checks the source app and processes transaction notifications from selected apps to identify financial entries. Selected app names, icons and package identifiers are used for the chooser and local filtering. Nidhi queries launchable installed apps for this selection; it does not send that installed-app list to a developer server.

Recognized financial fields and duplicate-detection information are processed locally. Complete raw notification text is not stored as an import archive or sent to the developer. Imported ledger details may later be included in backups or exports. Disable the switch or revoke Android notification access to stop future processing. Existing imported records are not deleted automatically.

Automatic recognition and duplicate matching are not guaranteed to identify every message correctly. Review imported entries in History and correct or remove inaccurate records.

4. Screenshots, camera and QR tools

When you select or share a payment screenshot, Nidhi reads the selected image to extract candidate transaction details for review. Text recognition uses Google ML Kit on your device. Your selected image and recognized text are not sent to a developer-operated OCR server.

Camera access is requested for scanning QR codes. File or image access is used when you choose content to import; older Android versions may request storage permission for image selection. Denying those permissions leaves manual transaction entry available.

QR transfer and sharing tools can reveal the records or payment information you choose to display or share. A person or app receiving a QR code, image or exported file can read its contents. Review the destination before sharing.

5. Google sign-in, backups and sharing

Google account access

Google sign-in and Drive connection use Google’s authorization screens. Nidhi receives the account information and access authorization needed for the selected feature; it does not receive your Google password. Drive access uses the drive.file scope for files created or opened through the app’s authorized workflow.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you connect Drive and request backup, your transaction records and supported monthly-plan information are uploaded to your Google Drive. Backup files and related sync metadata are stored in the app’s MoneyLedger_Backup folder. If you separately enable automatic backup, it can run in the background while your account remains connected.

Google processes account, connection and storage information under its own policies. You can disable automatic backup, disconnect Drive, or revoke the connection through your Google Account. Disconnecting does not delete existing Drive files.

Local exports and Android backup

You choose the destination for spreadsheet exports and the source for imports using Android’s file picker. A location may be local storage or another provider, including a cloud provider. That provider handles the file under its own policies.

Android’s system backup or device-transfer features may also copy Nidhi’s database and preferences when enabled on your device. These are separate from Nidhi’s optional Drive backup and are controlled through your Android and backup-account settings.

Exported spreadsheets and QR transfers are not encrypted by Nidhi. App lock does not encrypt these copies. Anyone with access to an exported file or displayed QR data may be able to read it. Keep sensitive copies in a location you trust.

We do not sell or rent your personal or financial information, use bank messages for advertising, or operate a service that receives your ledger for marketing or model training. Information is transferred to providers when needed for features you choose, such as Google sign-in, Drive backup or external sharing.

7. App lock and security

Biometric app lock is optional and off by default. When enabled on a device with a configured screen lock, Android verifies your fingerprint, compatible face authentication or device PIN/pattern/password. Nidhi receives the authentication result, not your biometric template or device-lock secret.

The default background grace period is one minute. You can choose Immediately, 30 seconds, 1 minute or 5 minutes in the app-lock card. Turning off the device screen invalidates the app session immediately. Authentication is also required when a new app process has no authenticated session. Short trips to a file picker can stay within the chosen grace period.

If the session has expired, import/export waits for successful unlock. An import that cannot meet the authentication check before committing is cancelled without partial database changes. Without a device screen lock, biometric protection cannot be activated; Settings explains how to configure one.

Ledger data is kept in app-private Android storage. App lock is an access control, not a claim of end-to-end encryption or separate database encryption. Device security, system backups, exported files, and your Google Account remain important. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Service providers and technical diagnostics

Nidhi uses Google Play services, Google Sign-In, Google Drive APIs and Google ML Kit for the features described above. Nidhi does not include its own advertising or behavioral-tracking system.

According to Google’s ML Kit documentation, input images and recognition results are processed on-device. ML Kit may contact Google for models or updates and send SDK usage, performance, app and device information for diagnostics and service improvement. This technical processing is separate from uploading your payment screenshots or ledger to the developer.

9. How long information remains

Local ledger entries, monthly plans, settings and import fingerprints remain until removed through the relevant controls or until the app’s storage is cleared. Revoking a permission stops future access but does not remove previously imported data.

Drive backups, exported files, copies shared with other apps, and Android backups can outlive the local app installation. They remain under the applicable account, file provider or recipient’s retention rules until removed there. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

10. Your choices, access and deletion

  1. Review or correct records: use History to view, edit or delete transactions.
  2. Stop automatic imports: disable SMS/notification reading in Nidhi and, if desired, revoke the corresponding Android permission or notification access.
  3. Delete transactions: use Settings → Data management → Delete all data and its confirmations. The current control deletes transactions; recurring plans, categories, preferences and local profile information may remain.
  4. Erase all local app information: use Android Settings → Apps → Nidhi → Storage → Clear storage/data. Menu names vary by device. This removes the app’s local database, profile and settings. Export anything you want to keep first.
  5. Remove cloud/file copies: disable automatic backup, delete the MoneyLedger_Backup folder or its files from Google Drive, and empty Drive’s bin if appropriate. Separately delete exports from Downloads, other file providers, recipients and system backups you control.
  6. Disconnect Google: use Nidhi’s Drive disconnect control and manage/revoke the Nidhi connection in your Google Account. Signing out or disconnecting does not itself erase your ledger or previously created backups, and does not delete your Google Account.

To request privacy or deletion assistance outside the app, email clef2k22@gmail.com with the subject Nidhi privacy or deletion request. We can help with controls and address information you have provided directly to support. Because we do not hold your local ledger on a developer server, an email cannot remotely erase your phone, your Google Account, or copies controlled by other providers.

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction or withdrawal of consent. Contact us to discuss a request. Withdrawing optional access does not undo processing already completed or automatically delete saved records.
