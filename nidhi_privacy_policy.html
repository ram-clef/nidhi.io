Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Offline Mode (default): You can use Nidhi without any account. You may optionally enter a display name — this stays locally and is never transmitted. No personal information is required.

Google Sign-In (Optional): If you choose to enable backup/restore via Google Drive, Nidhi uses Google Sign-In. When signed in, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture URL. Your transaction data remains on your device or your private Google Drive — never on our servers. You can sign out at any time, and all backup data remains under your Google account control.

Screenshot Data (Premium Feature): When you import payment receipts via screenshot, all OCR processing occurs entirely on your device. Images are never uploaded to any external server. Extracted information is only used to prefill transaction fields.

QR Code Device Transfer: You may transfer transaction data between devices using QR codes. This is a direct device-to-device transfer — no intermediate servers, no cloud storage.

No Analytics / Tracking: Nidhi does not embed any analytics SDKs or third-party tracking tools that collect personal information.

Use of Your Personal Data

We may use Personal Data only for: providing offline functionality, optional Google Backup (if enabled), improving stability, and responding to support requests. We do not sell or rent any information.

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share personal data with third parties except: when required by law, during a merger/acquisition, or with explicit consent. Authentication services (Google) process data according to their own privacy policies.

Retention & Deletion of Data

All financial transactions reside in local Room Database. You can delete individual transactions, use "Delete All Data" inside settings, or uninstall the app to permanently erase everything. Google Sign-In data is removed when you sign out.

Security of Your Data

Local data is stored using encrypted SQLite (Room). Since the app does not transmit financial records over the internet, the risk of exposure is minimal. For optional Google Drive backup, data is encrypted during transit using OAuth 2.0 standards.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to Other Websites

Nidhi may include links to external support resources (GitHub, email). We are not responsible for third-party privacy practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update the policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date will reflect changes. Continued use of Nidhi constitutes acceptance.

📦 Data Management & Safety

  • Local storage technology: Room Database (SQLite) with encryption.
  • Supported backup methods: Export to XLSX, QR code transfer between devices, and optional Google Drive backup (via Google Sign-In).
  • Import methods: XLSX file, QR code scan, on-device screenshot OCR.
  • Deletion capabilities: Single transaction delete, bulk delete, full reset from Settings → Delete All Data.
  • No third-party tracking SDKs: No Firebase Analytics, no Crashlytics, no Ads libraries.

Data Safety Section (for Play Store requirement)

Data not collected: Location, contacts, microphone, biometrics, health data, financial account credentials, browsing history.
Data encrypted in transit: Only for Google Sign-In OAuth flow and Google Drive backups (user's own cloud).
Data deletion policy: Users can permanently delete all data via in-app settings or by uninstalling the app.
